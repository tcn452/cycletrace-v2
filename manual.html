System Architecture & Security 02

CycleTrace is built with an immutable verification-first model designed for the realities of the South African cycling community. It bridges individual bicycle owners, independent bike shops, insurers, and law enforcement.

Technical Stack Overview

Layer Technology Functional Purpose
Frontend Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript Server components, dynamic streaming layouts, and responsive client interfaces.
Authentication Appwrite Auth, Session & Recovery API Encrypted credentials, persistent sessions, and self-service account recovery tokens.
Database Appwrite TablesDB & Local Synchronized Registry Bicycle records, transfer records, organization applications, and verification queues.
Storage Appwrite Storage Bucket (bikephotos) Encrypted storage for bicycle photographs with strict access permissions.
Onboarding OnboardJS React Tour Engine Interactive product tour with local storage persistence and session synchronisation.
Payments PayFast Integration South African debit/credit card and Instant EFT subscription processing.
POPIA Protection Data Minimisation Architecture Public serial validation without ever exposing personal contact details or addresses.

Security & Privacy Principles

  • POPIA Compliance: Public search results verify ownership authenticity and theft flags, but strictly withhold the owner's full name, phone number, and street address.
  • Role Separation: Riders, Store Owners, Insurers, and Admins operate in isolated workspaces. Elevated roles require admin verification before privileged commercial tools unlock.
  • Cryptographic Handover: Transfers utilize unique secure tokens tied to the designated recipient's email address to eliminate fraudulent serial claims.
  • Production Cleanliness: Demo bypasses and direct admin links are hidden from user-facing screens to maintain strict security and production integrity.

The public portal enables potential second-hand buyers, bike shops, and law enforcement to instantly verify a bike’s ownership and theft status before money changes hands.

Navigate to /. The homepage displays CycleTrace’s mission, verification badges, and a prominent search bar.

Side-by-Side Action: Scroll to the search input under "Before you buy, check the trace". Enter a brand, model, or frame serial number (e.g. Trek, Specialized, or WSBC) and press Enter or click Search registry →.

Theft Reporting & Recovery Workflow 07

When a bicycle is stolen, every hour counts. CycleTrace allows immediate status escalation so any buyer or bike shop checking the serial number is warned.

STEP 1 Access Theft Reporting (/dashboard/report)

Navigate to /dashboard/report from your dashboard action menu.

Side-by-Side Action: 1. Select the bicycle from the dropdown list of your owned bikes.
2. Enter the date of incident, last seen location, and police case number (SAPS CAS).
3. Add any distinguishing marks (e.g. scratched top tube, aftermarket pedals).
4. Click Flag as stolen →.
STEP 2 Verify Public Registry Escalation

CycleTrace updates the bike’s record status in Appwrite from protected to stolen instantaneously.

Side-by-Side Action: Open an incognito or private browser tab. Go to /search and search for the serial number of the bike you just flagged. Observe the high-visibility red REPORTED STOLEN alert banner on its public page.
Community Protection Mechanism: The public page includes a direct reporting action for anyone spotting the bike to securely submit tips to reports@cycletrace.co.za without exposing the owner's personal details.

Secure Peer-to-Peer Ownership Transfer Protocol 08

Selling or giving away a bicycle privately? CycleTrace enables verifiable cryptographic handovers, updating the provenance record and transferring management rights safely.

Two-Party Transfer Workflow

SENDER FLOW Initiating Transfer (/dashboard/transfer)
Side-by-Side Action: 1. On /dashboard, locate the bike card and click Transfer ownership.
2. Enter the buyer's email address (e.g. buyer@example.com).
3. Agree to release ownership rights and click Generate transfer link →.
4. Copy the unique transfer URL containing the secure handover token.
BUYER FLOW Accepting Ownership (/transfer/[token])
Side-by-Side Action: 1. Paste the transfer URL in a browser.
2. Review the bike details on the transfer reception page.
3. Enter the buyer email to verify authorization.
4. Click Accept ownership →.
Post-Handover State: The bike is immediately removed from the seller's active fleet and assigned to the buyer's account. The public provenance history logs the transfer timestamp.

Store Owner POS Stock & Commercial Paper Trail 09

For bicycle retailers and bike shops, CycleTrace provides a specialized commercial workspace (/store-owner). Store owners register showroom stock, transfer ownership to customers upon sale, and issue verifiable digital & printable transfer certificates.

Store Owner Workspace Structure (/store-owner)

  • Store Profile & Verification Status: Displays store trade name, physical address, business reg, and verified badge.
  • Inventory Metrics: Live count of Stock in store versus bikes Transferred to bikers.
  • Shop Stock Inventory Tab: Displays all bikes held in store stock with brand, model, serial, and a 1-click Transfer to biker action.
  • Customer Handover Paper Trail Log Tab: Complete historical ledger of every customer handover, showing Date, Bike, Frame Serial, Customer Name & Email, Invoice Number, and direct link to the Certificate.
STEP 1 Registering Stock into the Showroom
Side-by-Side Action: 1. On /store-owner, click Add stock bike.
2. Enter the bicycle make, model, frame serial number, color, and photos.
3. Submit the bike. It appears under the Shop Stock Inventory tab ready for floor display.
STEP 2 Customer Handover & Transfer Form (/store-owner/transfer)

When a customer purchases a bicycle, the shop owner initiates a verified retail transfer that binds the sale to an invoice reference.

Side-by-Side Action: 1. Click Transfer to biker on any stock bike card.
2. Enter the Customer's Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
3. Enter the Store Invoice / Receipt Ref (e.g. INV-2026-0412).
4. Set Handover Date and optional Warranty / Service notes.
5. Click Complete Transfer & Generate Paper Trail →.
STEP 3 Verifiable Transfer Certificate (/store-owner/transfers/[id]/certificate)

An official Certificate of Ownership Transfer is instantly generated. This serves as an audit-ready paper trail for the customer, store, and insurer.

Side-by-Side Action: 1. Click Print / Download Certificate from the completion screen or from the Paper Trail Log.
2. Inspect the official verification seal, unique Certificate Serial Number (e.g. CERT-CT-...), frame serial, invoice reference, and dual signature sign-off lines.
3. Use Ctrl+P / Cmd+P to print a clean, high-resolution physical handover sheet for the customer.

Frequently Asked Questions & Support 14

Common Questions

Where do I find my bike's serial number?

On 95% of bicycles, the serial number is stamped into the metal on the underside of the bottom bracket shell (beneath the pedals). It can also sometimes be found on the rear dropout or the head tube.

What is the difference between Peer Transfer and Store Stock Transfer?

Peer transfers (/dashboard/transfer) are for private individuals selling used bikes. Store Stock Transfers (/store-owner/transfer) are point-of-sale commercial handovers executed by verified retailers, capturing store till slip/invoice references and generating printable Certificates of Ownership Transfer with official serial numbers.

Can someone steal my bike by claiming my serial number?

No. Once a serial number is registered in CycleTrace, any attempt to register the same serial number triggers an automatic 409 Conflict guard. Ownership can only be updated through an authorized cryptographic handover link from the existing registered owner.

How does CycleTrace protect owner privacy under POPIA?

In accordance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), public search results only disclose bicycle technical specifications and theft verification status. Your full name, residential address, and contact numbers are strictly confidential.
